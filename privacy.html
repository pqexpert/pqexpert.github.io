Inquiry privacy

Share enough to start. Keep sensitive details out.

These forms help Josh respond to the conversation you choose.

What the forms collect

Email is required for a response. Name is optional. Professional, hiring, and author forms offer an optional short context field. The private-contact form has no message or upload field and does not request your timezone.

Where the information goes

The forms and response conversations use HighLevel / LeadConnector in the existing Restoration Security Consultants business account. Submission information, contact details, and related messages are used to acknowledge, route, and follow up on your request. A private-contact request uses that same system; it is not a separate confidential vault or a secure incident-reporting channel.

Your purpose stays yours

An inquiry does not subscribe you to marketing, newsletters, SMS, or a sales sequence. Hiring and editorial requests are not automatically commercial opportunities. Josh can coordinate a different channel when the subject needs one. Please do not submit credentials, controlled documents, personnel records, private sources, or sensitive customer or personal information.

Links and attribution

Public campaign labels in a link may be carried through the site to a non-private form so the source of an inquiry can be understood. The private route does not forward these labels. This site does not store form answers or add advertising pixels. The form provider and external services operate their own infrastructure and may process technical data needed to deliver their services.

Continuing or ending a conversation

Reply in your existing thread to correct your details, change how you want to be contacted, ask about stored information, or ask Josh to stop or remove information that is no longer needed. If you cannot use the thread, request contact without adding sensitive details. Legal or contractual recordkeeping needs, where applicable, are handled with the request rather than promising automatic deletion.

Reading and social platforms

Following The Restoration Ledger or an author on Medium stays with Medium. A follow, a social reaction, or an article visit is not treated as permission to import you into an email campaign.
