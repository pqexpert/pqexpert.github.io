# Contributing

PQExpert.io is a governed professional portfolio. Contributions must preserve technical quality, public safety, evidence discipline, and identity protections.

## Before proposing a change

- Confirm that the work is public-safe.
- Do not add protected legal identity information unless a governed public context explicitly requires it.
- Do not add credentials, secrets, private contact data, job-search records, clearance information, client information, or unpublished sensitive research.
- Do not convert aspirations, coursework, concepts, or roadmap items into experience claims.
- Identify the evidence and maturity label for every material claim.

## Maturity labels

Use one of these meanings consistently:

- **Verified:** supported by governed evidence suitable for public use.
- **In development:** active work exists, but production or adoption is not established.
- **Research direction:** exploration or proposed capability without implementation evidence.

## Pull-request checklist

- [ ] Claim source checked against governed records.
- [ ] Protected and sensitive information excluded.
- [ ] Provenance updated with the public claim.
- [ ] Internal links and referenced assets resolve.
- [ ] `python scripts/check_site.py` passes.
- [ ] Desktop and mobile rendering reviewed.
- [ ] Accessibility and plain-language impact considered.
- [ ] No active-clearance, customer, partnership, patent, deployment, or adoption implication was introduced without evidence.

## Writing style

Use controlled, executive-safe language. Prefer concrete actions, environments, tools, outcomes, limitations, and evidence over slogans. Explain specialist language when a hiring manager, policymaker, or partner may not share the same technical context.

## Licensing

The repository does not yet grant a general reuse license. Do not contribute third-party material unless its license and attribution requirements are understood and compatible with the eventual project licensing decision.
