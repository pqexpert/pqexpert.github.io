## Common HighLevel entry contract

Four native forms: professional, hiring, author/editorial, private contact. Email required; name optional; short non-sensitive context optional on the first three. Private contact has no message, upload, or timezone collection. All create a native conversation. No sticky contact, saved-progress modal, submission PDF, advertising pixel, or inferred marketing subscription is added.

The family handoff workflow listens only to these four forms and assigns Josh one follow-up task due the next business day at 9 AM account time. This is an internal queue target, not a public response-time promise. Re-entry supports a later genuine request; concurrent re-entry while an execution is active remains subject to HighLevel's native behavior. Review repeated submissions and merge redundant work, preserving submission evidence.

The acknowledgment is inquiry-only, sends no sensitive form contents back, respects native email DND, and invites a reply. It is not evidence of a personal response, a sale, or inbox delivery. A successful form confirmation means submission; workflow execution, message delivery and human response are separate receipts.

Existing RSC commercial handoff and retention workflows remain scoped to their own pipeline and event rules. Hiring, private and editorial submissions are not automatically sales opportunities. Later requests can have different purposes without erasing earlier relationships.

## Collection, conversion and firm context

Offer useful evidence before asking for details. Keep one primary CTA per traffic item. Match its promise to the destination and form purpose. Do not require a phone number, account creation, document upload or booking to begin. Provide a direct native-form fallback and a usable mobile layout.

Collect organization, public domain, role, problem, desired outcome, deadline and procurement context progressively in the conversation. Link to source evidence and confirm the company/contact relationship before reusing it. Store facts and dates distinctly from guesses. An organization record, scraped public listing, visit, download, social reaction or AI fit score is not a buyer, consent or qualified opportunity.

Attribution uses public campaign labels only: utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content and utm_term. Site code propagates a bounded allowlist through same-site links and into non-private forms without persistent browser storage. Never put names, addresses, emails, account IDs, private query contents or credentials into campaign values. Private-contact links and frames omit these labels. No claim of cookie-free provider infrastructure is made.

HighLevel preserves first and latest attribution on supported conversion events; validate attribution against an actual native submission before using it for a performance claim. Our explicit query propagation helps preserve source context; it does not prove analytics coverage of every page view or cross-device visit. See [HighLevel attribution guidance](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001219997-understanding-attribution-source).

Consent is purpose-specific. An inquiry requests a response, not marketing. Any future newsletter, SMS or campaign requires a separate, clear optional choice and a stored source/time/purpose record, with withdrawal honored. See [HighLevel consent guidance](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006325-how-to-get-customer-consent-or-track-legal-basis-to-process-your-lead-s-or-customer-s-personal-data).

## Communication loop and Distiller

At every meaningful touch, retain: purpose, source pointer, native record/thread, responsible owner, state, last meaningful inbound/outbound event, promised next action, agreed date and stop/suppression state. Do not create a new task for every short thank-you when one open task already covers the relationship. Native conversation unread state remains visible.

Josh responds personally and agrees the next step. Operators prepare context, preserve thread continuity, reconcile overdue or failed handoffs, and support delivery. An acknowledgment, delivered email, read receipt and personal response are distinct events. The published PQExpert Incoming Reply Alert listens only to Email replies associated with the PQExpert Inquiry Acknowledgment workflow. It sends Josh an internal app notification linked to the conversation, with no copied reply text, follower notification or new task. Re-entry is enabled and unread state is preserved. This does not claim coverage of every email, phone or social channel; the owning operator still reconciles its native queue. Never interpret a reply as permission for more marketing. See [HighLevel Customer Replied trigger](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000002677-workflow-trigger-customer-replied).

Distiller sifts and sorts changed, permitted operational evidence from the existing owners. Prioritize explicit customer commitments and deadlines, delivery/support failures, unanswered requests, source corrections, and capacity conflicts. Essentialize to one next action, owner, due date, source pointer and actual exception. Never move private inquiry contents, manuscripts, personnel or customer records into shared briefs or public repositories. Keep duplicates linked rather than amplifying them.

Silence is green only after a current successful check finds no due action or exception. Missing receipts, paused automation, failed reads, undelivered acknowledgments and unowned requests are unknown or degraded, not green. Preserve the existing Distiller cadence and sole designated Calendar writer; do not activate a second writer.

## Evidence and measurement

Current setup: four saved native forms with conversation creation, published family handoff and acknowledgment workflows, a scoped incoming-reply app alert, and deployed purpose-specific public routes. Configuration is not an end-to-end customer receipt. First natural submissions on each new form still supply the form → task → acknowledgment → reply evidence; do not manufacture leads to improve counts.

Measure where native evidence is available: inquiries by form/source; delivered/skipped/failed acknowledgments; time to personal response; unanswered requests; agreed next steps; qualified opportunities; closed/lost/declined reasons; actual delivery outcomes; agreed care dates; continuing relationships. Visitor-to-inquiry conversion needs a valid visitor denominator; no denominator means no conversion-rate claim. Email opens are weak engagement evidence, not a customer outcome.

The previous RSC readiness inquiry/reply receipts remain evidence for that route only. They do not prove the new family forms or social routes have completed real customer journeys. Root/www outreach cutover and recovery remain their separately tasked operations, with current status in the owning private record. The PQExpert family site release is independent.

## Recovery

Publish the static site through its existing branch → Site quality check → merge → Pages verification procedure. Keep the native forms available while correcting a site problem. Roll back with a reviewed revert or corrective commit, not a force push. An iframe load error is not a submission success; show the direct form fallback. Preserve anti-bot protections and DND. A provider asking for human verification is handled by the person submitting, not by disabling the protection.
