Commercial operating context

Restoration Security Consultants

PQExpert is the public front door. RSC is the commercial operating context behind scoped cybersecurity work when the engagement needs a company, contracting, delivery, or business-development layer.

Client routing rule: start on PQExpert, review the evidence here, and open the conversation here. You do not need to find or move to a separate RSC website before discussing a project.

How the layers fit

1. PQExpert

Public capabilities, evidence, project maturity, writing, verification, and intake. This is the canonical external route.

2. Fit and scope

Define the problem, decision, constraints, evidence needed, timeline, and safe engagement boundary before implying a packaged solution.

3. RSC delivery

When commercial work is appropriate, RSC becomes the operating entity for the scoped engagement or partner path.

Current Public-Safe Focus

In Development

RSC's public-safe focus is cybersecurity operations support, compliance and documentation automation research, post-quantum readiness planning, technical strategy, and security governance. Emerging product language remains conservative until deployment, certification, customer, contract, or other resolving evidence is separately verified.

  • Security operations modernization, telemetry, response workflow, and endpoint-governance support.
  • Cyber resilience and infrastructure decision support.
  • Post-quantum readiness planning, inventory questions, migration reasoning, and executive education.
  • Compliance automation and documentation discipline.
  • Technical-policy translation and executive briefing for complex technology decisions.
  • Prime, lab, research, enterprise, and government-adjacent partner-path development where appropriate.
No public claim is made here about active client deployments, production-certified products, patents, regulatory approvals, current contract awards, or active formal partnerships unless those claims are separately verified and approved for publication.
