What RSC Is

Restoration Security Consultants (RSC) is a Houston-based managed security services company founded in January 2020 by Josh Allen. RSC is focused on delivering AI-native cybersecurity, compliance automation, and post-quantum readiness research to small and mid-sized businesses in regulated industries.

RSC is the commercial and company-building layer. PQExpert.io is the personal professional portfolio layer. Josh Allen is the founder and operator of RSC — but the two identities serve different audiences and different purposes.

What RSC Is Building

⚡ In Development

RSC's R&D focus is orchestrator-first security architecture — a systematic approach to automating security operations through interconnected detection, threat management, and cryptographic systems.

  • CART (Cognitive Autonomous Response Triage) — the orchestrator as primary triage layer, not an assistant. Analysts review decisions, not raw alerts.
  • DTM (Dynamic Threat Management) — active R&D, private repository
  • PQEaaS (Post-Quantum Encryption as a Service) — active R&D through Fungible-RD
These are active R&D and development directions. RSC does not claim active customer deployments or production-certified products at this time.
