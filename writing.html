Why this belongs next to the technical portfolio

Writing is another form of evidence.

The point is not to look prolific. The writing should show how I frame ambiguity, distinguish facts from assumptions, connect technical and institutional layers, make an argument, and leave a reader with a clearer decision than they had before.

For hiring teams, policy professionals, research partners, and executives, that is part of the same capability demonstrated in the technical portfolio: understand the system, identify the important relationships, explain the consequences, and move toward action.
