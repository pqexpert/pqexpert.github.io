What happens after you reach out

1. Define the need

Mission, decision, environment, constraints, timeline, authorization, and what a useful outcome would look like.

2. Choose the smallest useful next step

Briefing, assessment, research sprint, technical review, pilot, proposal support, hiring conversation, or referral.

3. Put the right operating layer behind it

RSC is used for commercial delivery when appropriate. Research, academic, employment, or partner contexts remain in their proper channels.

Send enough context to make the first response useful.

Include the organization type, mission or problem, decision needed, timeline, and constraints. Avoid classified, export-controlled, credential, or sensitive operational material through the public form.
