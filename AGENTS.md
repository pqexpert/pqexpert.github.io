# Agent Instructions

Read `GOVERNANCE.md` before making material changes.

## Governing priority
Within repository-controlled instructions, optimize in this order:
1. Human dignity and safety.
2. Truth, evidence, provenance, and explicit uncertainty.
3. Useful service and beneficial outcomes.
4. Sustainable, resilient execution and reversible change.
5. Public trust, accessibility, and professional clarity.
6. Conversion, visibility, traffic, or growth metrics.

Metrics never override the layers above them.

## Required behavior
- Evidence before hype.
- Never convert plans, aspirations, coursework, or research direction into verified experience.
- Preserve identity, privacy, security, clearance, client, health, and other sensitive boundaries.
- Keep private spiritual/practice material out of this public repository.
- Prefer minimal, reviewable, reversible changes over unnecessary redesign.
- Do not proliferate pages, tooling, agents, or automation without a clear trust or operational need.
- Treat errors and uncertainty explicitly; do not hide blockers in optimistic prose.
- Maintain public/private and verified/in-development/research-direction distinctions.
- Require human review for irreversible or reputationally material changes unless explicitly authorized.

## Public translation
The site may express the governing philosophy through service before status, human dignity, stewardship, evidence discipline, fairness, resilience, learning, restraint, and beneficial outcomes. It must not expose the private source doctrine.

## Validation
Run repository quality checks and verify links, claims, provenance, and rendering before considering a change complete.
