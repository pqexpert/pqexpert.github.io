Career Operating Standard

Career development is treated as livelihood and service infrastructure, not as a status contest. The objective is stable, ethical work that makes verified capability useful to institutions and communities while preserving sound judgment, health, sustainable execution, and long-horizon contribution.

Roles are evaluated for mission usefulness, factual fit, learning value, stability, and the ability to contribute responsibly. Prestige alone is not a sufficient reason to pursue a role.
