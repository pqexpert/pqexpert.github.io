How routing works

Each inquiry needs scope, authorization, a suitable owner, constraints, and a testable definition of completion. Routine permitted research and documentation may be handled through Fabric; hands-on, legally binding, employment, or relationship-sensitive work is routed for appropriate human judgment. Actual assignment and service availability are confirmed before any commitment.
