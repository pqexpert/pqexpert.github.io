# Security Policy

## Scope

Security reports are welcome for the public PQExpert.io website and its deployment configuration.

Useful reports include:

- exposed secrets or credentials;
- unsafe forms, redirects, or third-party integrations;
- cross-site scripting or content-injection risks;
- privacy leaks or protected identity exposure;
- dependency or deployment-chain weaknesses;
- misleading security behavior that could put visitors at risk.

## Reporting

Do not place sensitive vulnerability details, personal information, credentials, or proof-of-concept payloads in a public issue.

Use the contact route at https://pqexpert.io/contact and state that the message concerns a security report. In the initial message, include only:

- the affected URL or component;
- a concise description of the impact;
- whether the issue appears actively exploitable;
- a safe way to continue the conversation.

Please wait for a private response channel before sending sensitive reproduction details.

## Response goals

The project will aim to:

- acknowledge a credible report within five business days;
- assess severity and exposure;
- contain urgent risks before broader remediation;
- communicate a remediation or follow-up plan when practical.

These are response goals, not a service-level guarantee.

## Research rules

Good-faith research must avoid:

- accessing, modifying, or retaining other people’s data;
- denial of service or resource exhaustion;
- social engineering;
- credential attacks;
- persistence or destructive testing;
- testing third-party services outside this project’s control.

## Supported version

The currently deployed default branch and live site are supported. Historical commits and external mirrors are not actively maintained.
