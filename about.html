About Josh Allen

Cybersecurity operator, infrastructure architect, CTO, founder, and active graduate researcher.

Josh Allen is a cybersecurity and systems-resilience operator whose work spans technical infrastructure, governance, secure environment design, education-linked cyber modernization, and public-facing thought leadership. PQExpert.io is his professional portfolio and public credibility layer — connecting verified experience, selected projects, active graduate research, and Restoration Security Consultants context in one governed place.

Currently serving as Chief Technology Officer at the Global Counter-Terrorism Institute (GCTI), where he leads multi-phase cyber operations modernization and lab infrastructure governance. Simultaneously active as a graduate researcher in cybersecurity and international security studies at the Paris Graduate School, and pursuing an Executive Master's in International Relations at Syracuse University. Policy Advisor at The Restoration Ledger, a publication bridging technology, human rights, and digital governance.

Josh works at the intersection of cyber readiness, infrastructure discipline, and workforce modernization — with a particular focus on how organizations translate emerging technology, including post-quantum cryptographic systems and AI-driven detection, into actual operational capability rather than pilots.

Identity note: Josh Allen is the public-facing professional identity for work across cybersecurity, infrastructure, education, and thought leadership. Jonathan Denning is the legal/governance identity for entity-level operations. Both reflect the same person and the same body of work.

How I Work

  • Evidence before hype. Claims are backed by verifiable work, not approximations or aspirations.
  • Disciplined documentation. Governance and execution records over scattered memory and tribal knowledge.
  • Security-first infrastructure thinking. Every system evaluated for how it handles failure, access, and adversarial conditions.
  • Ethical and accountable technology use. Technology serves mission. Mission serves people.
  • Claims aligned to maturity. Deployed, in-development, and research directions are always kept distinct.
  • Portfolio as proof, not performance. This site exists to convey real capability to real readers.
