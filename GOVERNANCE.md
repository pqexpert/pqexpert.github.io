# PQExpert.io Public Operating Charter

PQExpert.io is a governed professional proof surface. Its public purpose is to help serious readers evaluate useful capability, judgment, and evidence without turning professional visibility into an end in itself.

## Governing principles

1. **Service before status.** Work, education, publishing, and public credibility should increase the ability to protect people, strengthen institutions, reduce harm, and contribute usefully.
2. **Human dignity and safety.** Security, resilience, automation, and institutional design should protect people as well as systems.
3. **Evidence before hype.** Material claims stay close to governed records, artifacts, and clearly labeled maturity states.
4. **Stewardship over self-mythology.** Public work is treated as a contribution to larger missions, not as proof of personal centrality.
5. **Fairness and disciplined leadership.** Establish facts before assigning motive; distinguish people from systems; preserve good-faith relationships; escalate proportionately when coordination fails.
6. **Sustainable execution.** Capability that destroys health, attention, trust, or continuity is not durable capability. Prefer resilient systems, reasonable operating load, and recoverable failure modes.
7. **Learning and correction.** Unknowns stay explicit. Errors are corrected. Process failures should become opportunities to improve the system for the next person.
8. **Beneficial outcomes.** Output, visibility, revenue, and credentials are means. The relevant test is whether the work produces safer, clearer, more resilient, more humane outcomes.

## Public/private boundary

The public site translates deeper personal ethics into professional operating standards. It does not publish private spiritual practice, teacher relationships, vows, health information, protected identity material, client secrets, clearance-related information, or other sensitive context.

## Decision test

Before a material public claim, project, partnership, or expansion, ask:

> Does this increase trustworthy capability and beneficial service without sacrificing the human, ethical, and operational foundations that make the work sustainable?

If the answer is unclear, reduce scope, gather evidence, redesign, defer, or decline.

## Relationship to repository policy

This charter supplements `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and `SECURITY.md`. It does not relax evidence, identity, or security controls. When principles conflict with a public claim or implementation shortcut, the safer and better-supported interpretation governs.
