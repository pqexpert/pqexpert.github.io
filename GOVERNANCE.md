# PQExpert.io Public Operating Charter

PQExpert.io is a governed professional and client-facing proof surface. Its public purpose is to help serious readers evaluate useful capability, judgment, maturity, and evidence, then open the correct conversation without requiring them to navigate an unclear collection of brands or sites.

## Source and operating roles

The public architecture is intentionally explicit:

- **Notion governs** strategic state, decisions, approval boundaries, maturity state, priorities, and cross-system governance.
- **GitHub stores durable technical truth**: code, architecture, documentation, runbooks, tests, provenance, implementation evidence, recovery knowledge, and repository-local engineering state.
- **PQExpert projects the public-safe view** of capability, evidence, project maturity, writing, verification, and engagement routes.
- **Restoration Security Consultants (RSC) operates commercial work** when a scoped engagement requires a company, contracting, delivery, or business-development layer.

No dashboard, public page, AI assistant, or external builder may silently redefine implementation truth that belongs to a repository. No repository-local implementation may silently redefine governing purpose, maturity, or authorization that belongs to the governance layer.

## Client routing rule

PQExpert is the canonical public front door.

A client, partner, lab, prime, research organization, hiring team, or public-sector reader should be able to:

1. understand what the work is;
2. review relevant evidence and maturity labels;
3. choose an engagement path;
4. contact PQExpert directly;
5. be routed to RSC or another appropriate operating context only after the need is clear.

Do not force external readers to discover or switch to a separate RSC website before a serious conversation can begin. Entity, contracting, compliance, or due-diligence details are disclosed when the process actually requires them.

## Governing principles

1. **Service before status.** Work, education, publishing, public credibility, and commercial activity should increase the ability to protect people, strengthen institutions, reduce harm, and contribute usefully.
2. **Human dignity and safety.** Security, resilience, automation, and institutional design should protect people as well as systems.
3. **Evidence before hype.** Material claims stay close to governed records, repository evidence, artifacts, and clearly labeled maturity states.
4. **Stewardship over self-mythology.** Public work is treated as a contribution to larger missions, not as proof of personal centrality.
5. **Fairness and disciplined leadership.** Establish facts before assigning motive; distinguish people from systems; preserve good-faith relationships; escalate proportionately when coordination fails.
6. **Sustainable execution.** Capability that destroys health, attention, trust, or continuity is not durable capability. Prefer resilient systems, reasonable operating load, and recoverable failure modes.
7. **Learning and correction.** Unknowns stay explicit. Errors are corrected. Process failures should become opportunities to improve the system for the next person.
8. **Beneficial outcomes.** Output, visibility, revenue, and credentials are means. The relevant test is whether the work produces safer, clearer, more resilient, more humane outcomes.
9. **Repository-local durability.** Material implementation knowledge should live close to the code or system it governs, not only in chat, dashboards, or ephemeral workspaces.
10. **Minimum necessary disclosure.** Public proof should be strong enough for credible review without publishing secrets, protected identity, customer data, credentials, private practice material, or unnecessary operational detail.

## Maturity and claim states

Public work should use the strongest state that current evidence supports and no stronger:

- **Verified** — resolving evidence supports the material claim.
- **In Development** — active implementation or engineering direction exists, but the promoted public outcome is not yet fully resolved.
- **Research / Concept** — research, architecture, hypothesis, experiment, or planned work without sufficient implementation evidence for a stronger state.
- **Blocked / Not Public** — work exists but authorization, privacy, security, maturity, or evidentiary conditions prevent public promotion.

A repository, design, issue, branch, mockup, planned integration, or agent-generated artifact is not automatically evidence of production maturity.

## Documentation durability rule

Material systems should converge on repository-local documentation covering the applicable parts of:

- purpose and scope;
- architecture and trust boundaries;
- data and integration contracts;
- security and authorization boundaries;
- deployment, startup, shutdown, and health checks;
- backup, recovery, and rollback;
- tests and verification;
- current status and roadmap;
- limitations and unresolved assumptions;
- provenance and public/private claim boundaries;
- agent/automation governance where automated systems can act.

Notion may govern the decision that a change is required, approved, deferred, or blocked. GitHub should retain the durable technical artifact that makes the change understandable and recoverable.

## Public/private boundary

The public site translates deeper personal ethics into professional operating standards. It does not publish private spiritual practice, teacher relationships, vows, health information, protected identity material, client secrets, clearance-related information, credentials, secrets, unpublished sensitive research, or other sensitive context.

Private repositories may contain more operational detail, but they remain subject to need-to-know, authorization, secret-handling, client, privacy, and compartment boundaries.

## Decision test

Before a material public claim, project, partnership, repository promotion, or expansion, ask:

> Does this increase trustworthy capability and beneficial service without sacrificing the human, ethical, evidentiary, privacy, and operational foundations that make the work sustainable?

If the answer is unclear, reduce scope, gather evidence, redesign, defer, or decline.

## Relationship to repository policy

This charter supplements `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`, and the public Operating Principles page. It does not relax evidence, identity, authorization, or security controls. When principles conflict with a public claim or implementation shortcut, the safer and better-supported interpretation governs.
